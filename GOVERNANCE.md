# Governance — Toplink Y Viện

## §0 Execution gate

Before starting a work item, prove it is in scope, identify the canonical owner, acquire a
non-conflicting lease, and name its required evidence, human gates, and external target status.
An absent input is a blocker—not permission to infer facts or expand the task. Changes to scope
need the user’s explicit authorization and a dated entry in §4.

## §1 Scope lock

This repository owns the independent corporate Facebook Page track for Nhất Liệu Y Viện Toplink.
Its canonical scope is `docs Toplink/TOPLINK_PAGE_MASTER_PLAN.md`; its execution contract is
`docs Toplink/TOPLINK_PAGE_MILESTONES.md`.

### In scope

- TL-M0–TL-M9 artifacts, only in the declared order and after their dependencies are met.
- Evidence-grounded Facebook Page strategy, education, content system, short-form support,
  measurement, and operating handoff.
- DMP draft generation, Agency review, local documentation, and bounded verification.
- Project-local skills and agents that enforce these controls.

### Out of scope unless the user changes this section

- Diagnosis, treatment, cure, disease claims, or a substitute for professional care.
- Unverified franchise/legal language, product efficacy, service availability, prices, booking,
  contact, warranty, qualification, or testimonial claims.
- Paid acquisition, national conversion, a new primary channel, or reuse of a Thảo Tây asset.
- Page, Sheet, external-runtime, or publication mutation without explicit approval.

## §2 Deferred backlog

- Commercial CTAs after evidence-cleared offer, privacy, owner, SLA, price/conditions, and
  compliance inputs exist.
- TikTok or paid media as a primary operating channel after the canonical milestone gate permits it.
- National conversion after verified SKU, territory, logistics, legal, and fulfillment evidence.

## §3 Human inputs required

- Official Page URL/ID and dated baseline snapshot.
- Public/legal evidence for the Nhất Liệu Y Viện relationship and franchise wording.
- Product/service dossiers, offer menu, price/availability, and booking/contact/privacy flow.
- Production capacity, Toplink content start date, and professional reviewer.
- Approved Google Sheet spreadsheet ID, tab, range, schema, and bounded write authorization.

## §4 Scope-change and decision ledger

| Date | Decision/change | Authority | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-29 | Regenerate a standalone Claude ↔ Codex documentation scaffold based on reusable process patterns | User approval | No brand facts, external targets, history, staging data, credentials, or deliverables are imported |

### Change-control rules

- New ideas outside §1 go to §2; unresolved operational debt belongs in §2; required user-only
  information belongs in §3.
- A scope change must name the affected canonical owner, reason, date, and required re-validation.
- No agent may use convenience, partial completion, tool availability, or a previous brand’s setup
  as scope authorization.
