# Toplink knowledge brief

## Use

This is a compact, source-attributed context layer for agents and skills. It is not a substitute
for the canonical plan or milestone files.

## Confirmed operating foundation

- Use the names **Nhất Liệu Y Viện Toplink**, **Y Viện Toplink**, or **Toplink**; descriptor:
  “Y Viện Dưỡng Thân – Tỉnh Thức”; slogan: “Thân khỏe – Tâm an – Trí sáng”. Do not imply a
  hospital or use medical-cross styling. Source: Brand Profile §1.
- The positioning is proactive, regular body care combining Lý liệu, Dược liệu, and Dưỡng liệu;
  it is not a hospital, medical replacement, miracle treatment, beauty spa, or product-only shop.
  Sources: Brand Profile §§1–2; `01_positioning_and_pillars.md`.
- Voice: warm, calm, practical, knowledgeable, and transparent. Avoid mystical, alarming,
  coercive, overly clinical, or overpromising language. Sources: Brand Profile §10;
  `09_scripts_and_hooks.md`.
- Visual direction: refined New Chinese aesthetic, deep/wine red, brass, walnut brown, ivory,
  warm lighting, and real human-care/space imagery. Avoid stock-medical poses and miracle-machine
  imagery. Source: Brand Profile §12.

## Hypotheses and constraints

- Launch audiences and all performance expectations are hypotheses until evidence is collected.
- Hanoi is local awareness/service discovery when verified; nationwide is education/awareness only.
- Service names, duration, price, conditions, product efficacy, legal/public franchise language,
  Page identity, availability, contact, and qualifications remain blocked unless independently
  verified.
- Candidate territories are identity/limits, body literacy, operational proof,
  Lý–Dược–Dưỡng explanation, and founder/community journey. Final pillars require DMP validation.

## Mandatory safety summary

- Never diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, guarantee, or replace medical advice.
- Preserve individual variation; use support-level framing only when source-supported.
- Escalate concerning symptoms and higher-risk customers to professional advice.
- Do not retain unnecessary health information or PII; obtain documented testimonial/UGC consent.
