## 2. Cadence (greenfield, capacity-bounded)

- **No absolute dates.** Sequence is relative (`D-1 … D-28`); `TOPLINK_CONTENT_START_DATE` unset.

Shares approximate the pillar weights; health-sensitive buckets stay item-gated.

## 4. Engagement playbook (fail-closed)

- Comments/inbox about fit/safety → answer with support-level framing + disclaimer; escalate
  concerning/acute symptoms to professional advice, **never** to a conversion path.
- Requests for "chữa khỏi"/price/booking → do not promise; hold at non-commercial CTA until offer gate.
- No collection of unnecessary health info/PII; testimonial/UGC only with documented consent.
- No auto-DM funnels, no lead capture, no booking link until offer + privacy + compliance PASS.

## 5. CTA policy (before offer gate)

Allowed: **theo dõi Page · lưu bài · chia sẻ nội dung.** Booking/tư vấn CTA opens only with exact
offer/service, eligibility, location, availability, contact ownership, response SLA, privacy notice,
price/condition (if mentioned), and compliance verdict (master plan §11).

## 6. Growth tactics (organic only)

- Trust-led reach via operational-proof + educational content; save/share as primary spread signal.
- Reels for discovery; Hà Nội geo-relevant content for local lane, national education for `TL-A04`.
- **No paid boost** until `TL-M8` scope/budget gate. Cross-post founder = item-approval external action.
