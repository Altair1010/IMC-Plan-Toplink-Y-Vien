# YV_03_CONTENT_SYSTEM Target Content

## Content Territories

| Territory | Current objective | Evidence rule | Current priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dry Run & Standardization | Turn real practice, correction, and learning into useful stories | Only current events or explicit capture needs | Highest |
| KTD Taking Shape | Show what is currently being completed or prepared | Current visual or operational evidence required | High |
| KTD Space | Explain a verified detail of space through its intended experience | Current media and fact source required | Pending evidence |
| KTD People | Show human effort and current working standards | Current people and standard source required | Pending evidence |
| Thân – Tâm – Trí | Express the owner-approved philosophy consistently | Exact meaning pending | Pending evidence |
| Everyday Dưỡng Sinh | Provide bounded lifestyle education without service-result claims | Avoid diagnosis and outcome promises | Medium |
| Founder Journey | Connect a real event to a sourced founder reflection and brand value | Current founder facts required | Selective / pending evidence |

Percent allocation is intentionally not fixed. Priorities follow current business state,
available material, and observed audience response.

## Dry Run Intake Contract

Capture date, real event, discovery, correction, communication value, story angle,
suitable format, pipeline decision, and notes in ordinary human language. A row is not
treated as a current event unless a human records it or a current source supports it.

## Content Grammars

1. Before → Issue → Adjustment → Better.
2. Detail → Meaning.
3. Behind the Scene → Human Effort.
4. Real Event → Founder Reflection → Brand Value.
5. Process → Experience, without efficacy claims.

## Content Item Contract

One row represents one real item or one explicit planned capture need. Default A/B/C
variants are prohibited. Each item must connect to a current event or capture requirement,
one territory, one format, an operational CTA, a human-readable status, and later response
and learning fields.
