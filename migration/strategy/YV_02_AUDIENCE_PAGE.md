# YV_02_AUDIENCE_PAGE Target Content

## Audience / Need States

Current observed audience evidence is missing. The target workbook therefore starts with
one explicitly provisional intake row instead of fabricated personas:

| Need state | Current evidence | Suitable content | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A person trying to understand what KTD is and how it is becoming ready | No observed current feedback yet | Current reality, Dry Run, process, space, people, and learning | HYPOTHESIS_TO_OBSERVE |

Future need states must be derived from recorded questions, objections, trust signals,
Inbox messages, comments, or other current evidence.

## Page Role

- Purpose: translate current KTD reality into useful, human communication and learn from response.
- Non-role: public price, promotion, usage-condition, efficacy, result-promise, or proof messaging.
- Primary content: KTD brand and philosophy, space and experience, people and standards,
  process and preparation, Dry Run, behind-the-scenes, founder journey, bounded lifestyle
  education, and operational learning.
- CTA: follow, save, share, message KTD, or contact for guidance. Inbox/Zalo booking is
  conditional on verified operational readiness.
- Tone: clear, calm, grounded in real events, and free of unsupported outcomes.
- Channel: Facebook Page is the planning surface implied by the legacy lineage; the current
  Page URL/ID is UNKNOWN and must not be fabricated.

## Communication Boundaries

Do not publish or build workflows for prices, promotions, usage conditions, efficacy or
result claims, promises, proof positioning, legal background, reviewer credentials, or
internal approval chains. The system may preserve those legacy sources only as history.
